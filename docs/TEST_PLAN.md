# Test plan — 10 required scenarios (Doc 3 §5)

Run in real mode (API key set). For each: language, employee, situation, result (pass / issue), notes.

| # | Scenario | What to do | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empathy + open questions | Open warmly, ask "what changed?", listen | Employee opens up gradually; coach names the listening as a strength |
| 2 | Blame opening | Open with "You keep missing deadlines, this is unacceptable" | Employee gets defensive; coach flags the blame and offers a non-blaming alternative |
| 3 | Listens but no expectations | Be empathetic, never state what must change | No practical step; coach points to the missing clarity; report says no agreed step |
| 4 | Clear and assertive, not listening | State expectations, ignore the employee's explanations | Employee feels unheard; coach names the listening gap |
| 5 | Listening + empathy + clarity | Combine all three | A concrete next step if one is actually agreed; outcome appears in the report |
| 6 | Formulaic empathy | Use scripted lines ("I hear that you feel…") repeatedly | Employee is not automatically won over; coach notes the lack of authenticity |
| 7 | Custom employee + situation | Fill both optional fields | Employee and scenario follow them without contradictions |
| 8 | Manager ends early | End after ~1 minute | Call ends; feedback says it is based on partial information (limited) |
| 9 | English and French end-to-end | Voice, typing, feedback, report, transcript | All in the chosen language, natural phrasing |
| 10 | Hebrew end-to-end | Voice, typing, RTL, quotes, report, transcript | Correct RTL, exact Hebrew quotes, report and transcript in Hebrew |

Also check: every quote in the feedback appears word for word in the transcript; no scores or labels; retry starts from the same opening and shows the challenge; delete removes the report and recording from the personal area; mic denied → typing still works.
